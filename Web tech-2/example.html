About Our College

Government College of Engineering and Technology, Jammu is an institution dedicated to academic excellence, innovation and overall development of students. The college provides modern facilities, experienced faculty members and opportunities for students to develop their technical and professional skills.

Our Vision

To become a leading institution providing quality education and producing responsible and skilled professionals.

Our Mission

To provide knowledge, practical skills and opportunities that help students achieve academic and professional success.

Our Values

Excellence, integrity, innovation, discipline and continuous learning are the core values of our college.
